# Geographic provenance

National/administrative geometry: geoBoundaries SAU ADM1, boundary year 2017, release 9469f09. Source: OpenStreetMap / Wambacher. License: Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0, attribution © OpenStreetMap contributors. Source metadata retained in `docs/geoboundaries-metadata.json`.

- https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/SAU/ADM1/
- https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/SAU/ADM1/geoBoundaries-SAU-ADM1_simplified.geojson
- https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

`public/data/saudi-regions.json` is a simplified derived database under ODbL 1.0. It has rounded coordinates and omits very small islands for rendering. It is provided alongside the source and transformation script; it is not a legal boundary reference. Non-MVP polygons are background geography only; no content or routes exist for them.

Visualization layers are intentionally schematic. Points and paths are approximate visual placement, not claimed scientific coordinates. Elevations are artistic, not DEM data. No heights/distances are exposed as measurements.

- Western highlands: https://saudipedia.com/جغرافيا-السعودية and https://saudipedia.com/جبال-السروات
- Northern sand area: https://saudipedia.com/صحراء-النفود-الكبير
- Southern sand area: https://saudipedia.com/جغرافيا-السعودية
- Oases/agriculture: https://saudipedia.com/واحة-الأحساء and https://saudipedia.com/مدينة-بريدة
- Coastal paths follow the western/eastern sides of the national boundary at exhibition scale.
- Heritage indicators correspond only to Diriyah, historic Jeddah, and Rijal Almaa from approved DOCX content; no architectural models are invented.

The full-country geographical layers do not open or implement content for additional regions.
